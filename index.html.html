Frequently asked questions

Do I need to already know Hangul before starting?

No — if you're brand new, your first lessons cover Hangul itself before moving into grammar. See the curriculum section above for details.

What happens during a typical lesson?

We watch a short clip from a Korean show together, unpack the real expressions in it, and practice using them — with plenty of room for your questions along the way.

Do you provide lesson materials, or do I need to prepare anything?

Everything's provided. Each sentence we cover gets added to your personal Google Doc the same day, with English explanations included.

What if I need to miss or reschedule a lesson?

You can reschedule up to 48 hours before your lesson, once per month. See the full booking policy below for details.

Will I get notes after each lesson?

Yes — every lesson's notes go into your own Google Doc the same day, so you always have something to review.

What if I don't finish the mini challenge between lessons?

Totally fine — it's there to help, not to pressure you. We'll just pick up wherever you are.

What app do we use for lessons?

Zoom, with a link sent after your booking is confirmed.

Do you teach children?

No — lessons are designed for adult learners only.

How quickly will you respond to messages?

I reply to Instagram DMs and emails within 24 hours.
